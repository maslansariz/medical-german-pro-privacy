Medical German Pro - Data Deletion Request

This page explains how users of Medical German Pro, published by Maslan Sariz, can request deletion of data associated with the app.

How to request data deletion

Medical German Pro does not require users to create an account or sign in. If you still want to request deletion of data associated with Medical German Pro, follow these steps:

  1. Open the Google Play Store listing for Medical German Pro.
  2. Use the developer contact email shown on the store listing.
  3. Send a deletion request with the subject: Medical German Pro data deletion request.
  4. In the message, include the app name and the type of data you want deleted. Do not send sensitive medical information.

Delete local app data from your device

Learning progress, favorites, selected category, language, theme, and similar app preferences are stored locally on your device. You can delete this local data at any time:

  1. Open Settings on your Android device.
  2. Go to Apps.
  3. Select Medical German Pro.
  4. Open Storage.
  5. Tap Clear data, or uninstall the app.

Data deleted

The following app data can be deleted from the user's device by clearing app data or uninstalling the app:

  • learning progress and quiz history;
  • favorite terms;
  • selected category;
  • language and theme preferences;
  • locally stored app settings.

Data retained

Medical German Pro does not create user accounts and does not store account profile data on its own server. Some data may be retained or processed by Google services used by the app, including Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Android speech recognition. This data is handled according to Google's policies and the user's Google account settings.

Purchase records may be retained by Google Play for billing, tax, fraud prevention, refund, and legal compliance purposes according to Google Play policies. Analytics and advertising data may be retained by Google according to Google's retention settings and policies.

Additional retention period

Local app data is removed from the device immediately when the user clears app data or uninstalls the app. Deletion requests sent to the developer contact email are reviewed and handled within 30 days where applicable. Data retained by Google services follows Google's own retention periods.
