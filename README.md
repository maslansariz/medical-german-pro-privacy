# Privacy Policy

**Medical German Pro** is an educational Android app for learning German medical terminology.

This Privacy Policy explains what data the app processes, why it is processed, and which third-party services may receive data.

## Information we process

The app does not require a user account and does not ask you to enter personal profile information.

The app may process the following information:

- **Learning progress and preferences:** quiz history, learned terms, selected category, favorites, language, theme, and purchase status are stored locally on your device so the app can show your progress and remember your settings.
- **Microphone input:** if you choose to use the pronunciation feature, Android's speech recognition service may process your spoken input to convert speech to text. The app uses the recognized text only to evaluate pronunciation and learning progress.
- **App usage and diagnostics:** Firebase Analytics may collect app interaction and device information to help understand feature usage and improve app quality.
- **Advertising data:** Google AdMob may collect or receive device identifiers, ad interaction data, and approximate usage information to show and measure ads.
- **Purchase data:** Google Play Billing processes purchases and subscriptions. The app receives purchase status so it can unlock premium features.

## How we use information

Information is used to:

- provide learning, quiz, pronunciation, puzzle, and dictionary features;
- save your progress and preferences;
- unlock purchased premium features;
- show and measure ads where applicable;
- understand app usage and improve reliability and usability.

## Third-party services

The app uses Google services that may process data under their own privacy policies:

- Google Play Services
- Firebase Analytics
- Google AdMob
- Google Play Billing
- Android speech recognition services

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by the third-party services listed above as necessary to provide analytics, advertising, billing, and speech recognition functionality.

## Data retention and deletion

Learning progress and preferences are stored on your device and can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Data processed by Google services is handled according to Google's privacy policies and your Google account settings.

## Children's privacy

The app is intended for medical terminology learning and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes or when legal or platform requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page.

## Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact email listed for Medical German Pro on Google Play.
