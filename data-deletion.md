# Data Deletion Instructions

**App:** Medical German Pro

Medical German Pro does not require users to create an account or sign in.

## Delete local app data

Learning progress, favorites, selected category, language, theme, and similar app preferences are stored locally on your device. You can delete this data at any time by using Android system settings:

1. Open **Settings** on your Android device.
2. Go to **Apps**.
3. Select **Medical German Pro**.
4. Open **Storage**.
5. Tap **Clear data** or uninstall the app.

This removes the app's locally stored learning progress and preferences from your device.

## Request deletion

If you want to request deletion of data associated with Medical German Pro, contact the developer using the contact email listed for Medical German Pro on Google Play.

Because the app does not create user accounts, please include enough information in your request to help identify the issue, such as the app name and the type of data you want deleted. Do not send sensitive medical information.

## Third-party services

Medical German Pro uses Google services such as Firebase Analytics, Google AdMob, Google Play Billing, and Android speech recognition services. Data processed by these services is handled according to Google's privacy policies and your Google account settings.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
